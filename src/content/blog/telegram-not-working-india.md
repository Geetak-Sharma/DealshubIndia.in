## Why Telegram Is Not Working in India

Over the past few days, a significant surge of Telegram users in India have reported that the app is failing to establish a connection. Users on **Jio, Airtel, Vi, and BSNL** mobile networks, as well as home broadband connections, have experienced varying degrees of downtime.

While Telegram has not officially reported any global server outages, the issue appears to be localized routing or DNS lookup failures on specific Indian ISP (Internet Service Provider) gateways. In simple terms, your network provider is unable to route your traffic to Telegram's servers properly.

## Common Symptoms of Telegram Connectivity Issues

Depending on your network provider and location, you might face one or all of the following symptoms:

* **Stuck on "Connecting..." or "Updating...":** The progress bar at the top of the app spins indefinitely, and you cannot see new messages.
* **Media Not Loading:** Photos, videos, voice notes, and PDF files refuse to download, showing a loading circle forever.
* **Messages Not Sending:** Sent messages are stuck with a single "clock" icon instead of ticks, showing they haven't reached Telegram's servers.
* **Channels and Groups Not Opening:** Public search, group chats, and broadcast channels fail to refresh or load any content.

## Additional Fixes If Telegram Still Doesn't Work

If your Telegram app is still struggling to connect even after setting up the MTProto proxy, try these troubleshooting steps:

1. **Clear Telegram Cache:** Go to *Settings > Data and Storage > Storage Usage* and tap *Clear Cache*.
2. **Update your App:** Ensure you are running the latest version from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
3. **Change DNS Settings:** Set your device DNS to Google DNS (`8.8.8.8` / `8.8.4.4`) or Cloudflare DNS (`1.1.1.1`).
4. **Toggle Airplane Mode:** Turn Airplane Mode on for 10 seconds, then turn it off to refresh your network towers.
5. **Switch Network:** If you are on mobile data, try connecting to a Wi-Fi connection, or vice versa.
