Every year, India's two e-commerce giants—Amazon and Flipkart—go head-to-head with their massive festive sales. For a deal hunter, this is the most important time of the year. But where should you spend your hard-earned money?

We've analyzed data from the last 3 years to give you the definitive answer.

## 1. Electronics and Gadgets
**Winner: Tie**

- **Amazon**: Historically better for international brands like Apple (iPhones often hit all-time lows here), Samsung, and premium laptops. Amazon’s 'Diamond' members often get early access to the best price drops.
- **Flipkart**: Dominates the budget and mid-range smartphone segment. If you're looking for POCO, Realme, or Motorola, Flipkart almost always has a slight edge on pricing.

## 2. Fashion and Lifestyle
**Winner: Flipkart (Myntra integration)**

While Amazon Fashion is great, Flipkart’s deep integration with Myntra gives them a massive catalog advantage. During the *Big Billion Days*, fashion discounts often go up to 80-90% on top-tier brands like Nike, Adidas, and Levi's.

## 3. Bank Offers and Cashback
**Winner: Amazon (Amazon Pay ICICI)**

- **Amazon**: The constant 5% unlimited cashback for ICICI Amazon Pay cardholders is a game-changer. During sales, they usually partner with SBI or HDFC for instant 10% discounts.
- **Flipkart**: Usually partners with Axis Bank or ICICI for 10% instant discounts. While great, the Amazon Pay ecosystem feels slightly more cohesive for regular shoppers.

## 4. Delivery Speed and Reliability
**Winner: Amazon (Prime)**

Amazon Prime's delivery speed, especially in Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities, remains unmatched. During sale periods, Flipkart's delivery times can sometimes stretch due to the sheer volume of orders.

## 5. The 'Loot' Potential
**Winner: Dealshub (Our recommendation!)**

The real loots—those 90% off pricing errors—happen on **both** platforms. They are usually fixed within minutes.

- Buy **Premium Electronics** (iPhone, MacBooks, Sony TVs) on **Amazon**.
- Buy **Budget Phones & Fashion** on **Flipkart**.
