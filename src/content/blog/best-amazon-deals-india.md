Finding a **Loot Deal** (a price so low it feels like a mistake) is the ultimate high for any smart shopper. At Dealshub, we've been tracking these loots since 2018. 

Here are the top 7 secret tips to help you find them before they expire:

## 1. Monitor the 'Price Error' Window
Most loots happen due to manual entry errors or system glitches. These usually last only 5-15 minutes. Join our Telegram alerts to catch these in real-time.

## 2. Collect 'Hidden' Coupons
Amazon often has checkboxes for coupons that many people miss. Always look just below the price for a green text that says "Apply Coupon".

## 3. Use Price History Trackers
Never trust the "Original Price" shown by retailers. Use tools to see the actual price history and ensure you're getting a genuine all-time low.

## 4. The Lightning Deal Refresh
Lightning deals are refreshed every hour. The best ones (like 90% off electronics) are usually fully claimed within seconds. Add to waitlist if you miss one!

## 5. Bank Offers + Amazon Pay Balance
Maximize savings by combining lightning deals with specific bank cards (SBI/ICICI/HDFC) and using Amazon Pay rewards.

## 6. Check 'Warehouse' Deals
For heavy discounts on open-box items, Amazon Warehouse is a goldmine. These products are fully verified by Amazon and come with a warranty.

## 7. Follow Certified Deal Scouts
Scouring the web yourself is exhausting. Follow dedicated channels like **DealshubIndia** where we do the heavy lifting for you.
