## **Expected iPhone 17 Series Pricing**

The iPhone 17 is set to be one of the star attractions of both sales. However, keep in mind that the base model iPhone 17 now starts at 256GB. Due to global chip shortages and general pricing hikes, we might not see the classic sub-₹50,000 or sub-₹60,000 deals this time around.

### **Amazon Prime Day Expected Prices:**
*   **iPhone 17 (Base Model):** Expected to drop to **₹75,000 or lower** on the listing page. After applying bank discounts, the final price is expected to land between **₹69,900 and ₹73,000** (MRP ₹82,900).
*   **iPhone 17 Pro Max (256GB):** Expected to drop to a range of **₹1,35,000 to ₹1,40,000**.

### **Flipkart GOAT Sale Expected Prices:**
*   **iPhone 17 (Base Model):** Expected to drop to **₹65,000 to ₹70,000** after applying credit card discounts. 
*   **Pro Tip:** If the iPhone 17 touches ₹65,000, it is an absolute buy. If it stays closer to ₹69,000, it might be wiser to wait for the Big Billion Days (BBD) sale later in the year.
